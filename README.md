# OnHairAgent

Reusable ON Hair & Beauty Burmese-video and distribution workflow, campaign knowledge, reports, and local video assets.

## Start here

1. Pull the repository with `git pull --ff-only` at the beginning of every session.
2. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), [skills.md](skills.md), and the relevant campaign record before acting.
3. Complete only the requested stages. June is the HeyGen avatar; speech and explanatory text must be Burmese. Download the finished video locally.
4. Review, commit, and push new or changed relevant project files before ending the session. Preserve unrelated work and never force-push.

## Knowledge and assets

- [Workflow skill](skills.md): HeyGen generation, local download, Facebook distribution, GBP gallery uploads and separate Burmese Updates, verification, and Git synchronization.
- [Facebook campaign record](workflow-records/2026-09-22.md): source Reel, destination IDs, caption, and eight live / three pending / three blocked results.
- [GBP campaign record](workflow-records/2026-09-23-gbp.md): eight branch IDs, gallery uploads, published Updates, and the separate compliant video derivative.
- [Engagement audit](facebook-engagement-audit-2026-09-05.md) and [CSV](facebook-engagement-audit-2026-09-05.csv): historical campaign metrics and interpretation limits.
- `output/pdf/`: English and Chinese engagement-audit reports.
- `work/`: retained campaign MP4s, including the full June export and its 24.6-second GBP derivative. Older filenames are retained without assuming their content or campaign identity.

Statuses are dated observations, not current platform guarantees. Do not repeat completed campaigns or retry pending submissions automatically. Business claims and current group/profile permissions must be checked when needed.

## Local setup and portability

The canonical skill is `skills.md`. For Codex discovery, copy its content to a local `heygen-burmese-facebook-workflow/SKILL.md` in the user's skills directory. The checkout remains the source of truth for records and media; resolve relative paths from it. Historical absolute paths identify the original machine only.

The skill also names optional installed Facebook-outreach and business-profile skills used on the original computer. Their absence does not provide permission to guess destinations or bypass current tool requirements; the core procedure, campaign destinations and results are retained here.

This repository is public. Unrelated personal career documents, caches, temporary files, and credentials are excluded. Saving instructions does not itself authorize generating or publishing another campaign.
